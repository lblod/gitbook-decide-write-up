# Ethical Data Sharing Agreement

## Ethical Data Sharing Agreement for the local decision and legislation data space in the scope of the DECIDe-project.

This Ethical Data Sharing Agreement is a draft document and remains subject to legal validation and review. The provisions of this Agreement shall be interpreted and applied in accordance with applicable European, national and local legislation.

### 1. Purpose and Scope

This Ethical Data Sharing Agreement ("Agreement") establishes the principles, responsibilities, safeguards and conditions governing the sharing and use of data within the local decision and legislation data space established in the scope of the DECIDe project.

The Agreement is intended to promote lawful, fair, transparent and accountable data sharing while supporting innovation, research, public governance, interoperability and public benefit. The Agreement also aims to protect individual rights, privacy, democratic values and the public interest.

This Agreement applies to all participants involved in the data space, including data providers, data consumers, public authorities, researchers, businesses and technical partners that access, use, share, distribute or create derivative works from data made available through the data space.

Data may include open government data, public records, semantic enrichments, AI-generated annotations, provenance information, operational data, personal data where legally permitted, and any future data categories made available through the data space governance framework.

Data shall only be shared for legitimate and documented purposes. Permitted purposes include local government transparency, policy analysis, semantic enrichment, mobility analysis, sustainability reporting, decision discoverability, human validation and research related to public governance. Any new purpose shall be documented and approved through the DECIDe governance process.

### 2. Compliance with Law and Regulatory Framework

All data sharing and reuse activities shall comply with applicable European, national and local legislation.

This Agreement complements and shall be interpreted consistently with:

* GDPR (EU 2016/679);
* Data Governance Act (EU 2022/868);
* Data Act (EU 2023/2854);
* AI Act (EU 2024/1689);
* any other applicable legislation.

No data shall be shared where such sharing would violate applicable legal obligations.

Nothing in this Agreement authorizes activities prohibited by law. Participants remain responsible for complying with all relevant legal, regulatory and ethical obligations.

### 3. Ethical Principles

Participants agree to adhere to the following principles.

* **Transparency**: Participants shall be transparent regarding data sources, provenance, AI involvement, validation status, processing activities, dataset limitations and confidence levels where available. DECIDe's provenance framework shall serve as the primary mechanism for explainability, traceability and accountability.
* **Public Benefit**: Participants shall prioritize uses that contribute to societal, scientific, educational, governmental or economic benefit.
* **Privacy and Data Minimisation**: Only data necessary for the intended purpose shall be shared. Participants shall prefer metadata, aggregated data, pseudonymized data and anonymized data before sharing identifiable personal data.
* **Fairness and Non-Discrimination**: Participants shall not use data to support unlawful discrimination, exclusion or unfair treatment of individuals or communities. Participants shall actively monitor AI outputs for bias and inaccuracies.
* **Accountability and Human Oversight**: Participants accept responsibility for decisions and outcomes resulting from their use of the data. Human oversight shall be maintained throughout all AI-supported processes.
* **Integrity**: Participants shall preserve the accuracy, meaning and context of data when creating, sharing or publishing derivative works.

### &#x20;5. Data Protection, Privacy and Security

Where personal data is processed, participants shall document the lawful basis, retention periods, access controls and affected data subjects.

Participants shall implement privacy-by-design principles including, where appropriate, separation of AI annotations from source data, pseudonymous validation processes, controlled authorization mechanisms and limited retention periods.

Participants shall provide mechanisms supporting applicable rights relating to access, rectification, deletion, objection and portability in accordance with the governance arrangements of the data space.

Participants shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including role-based access control, encryption in transit, secure authentication, audit logging and incident response procedures.

Security incidents affecting shared data shall be reported without undue delay.

### 6. Permitted and Prohibited Uses

Participants may access, analyze, reproduce, redistribute and reuse data, create derivative datasets, develop software, visualizations, research outputs, educational materials and public services, and use data for commercial or non-commercial purposes unless restricted by law or dataset-specific conditions.

Participants shall not attempt to re-identify individuals; misrepresent or falsify data; present modified data as official information without disclosure; facilitate harassment, identity theft or unlawful activities; engage in unlawful surveillance; perform social scoring; conduct discriminatory profiling; manipulate democratic processes; implement automated decisions producing legal effects without human oversight;use the data in ways inconsistent with European fundamental rights.

### 7. AI, Provenance and Transparency

AI systems shall support rather than replace democratic decision-making.

Where data are used to train, validate, evaluate or operate AI systems, participants shall assess potential bias, evaluate societal impacts, document limitations and maintain human oversight.

Participants shall document when content is AI-generated, AI-assisted or AI-classified.

AI outputs shall remain traceable to relevant source documents, processing activities, reviews, validations, prompts where appropriate and model versions through the DECIDe provenance framework.

Organizations publishing analyses, applications or derived products shall disclose: original data sources; significant processing steps; AI involvement; assumptions used; known limitations; provenance availability; validation status where applicable.

### 8. Data Quality and Responsible Reuse

Participants shall cite original data sources whenever practicable, indicate retrieval dates, distinguish original information from derived information and avoid implying endorsement by the original data provider.

Participants shall promote trustworthy data sharing, interoperability, transparency regarding access rights and responsible reuse practices.

Data made available through the DECIDe Data Space should clearly indicate source organization, publication date, validation status, AI involvement, applicable licensing conditions and provenance availability.

### 9. Governance, Monitoring and Reporting

Participants are encouraged to report misuse, privacy vulnerabilities, security incidents, data quality concerns and potential ethical harms.

DECIDe shall establish an Ethical Data Governance Board within the Data Space Governance Board responsible for reviewing new datasets, AI use cases, high-risk processing activities, complaints and ethical concerns.

Participants commit to continuous monitoring of data quality, AI performance, validation outcomes, security incidents, model drift and ethical risks.

### 10. Disclaimer, Suspension and Termination

Data are provided "as is" without warranties regarding completeness, accuracy, fitness for purpose or continued availability.

Users remain responsible for assessing the suitability of the data for their intended use.

Access may be suspended or revoked where continued use presents legal, ethical or security risks or where participants materially breach this Agreement.

### 11. Acceptance

By accessing, downloading, or using the shared data, users acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Ethical Open Data Sharing Agreement and its guiding principles of openness, responsibility, fairness, and respect for individual rights.
