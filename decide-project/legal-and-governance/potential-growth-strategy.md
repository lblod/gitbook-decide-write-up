# Potential growth strategy

DECIDE has successfully demonstrated the feasibility of establishing a cross-border Local Decisions and Legislation Data Space connecting local governments in Belgium and Germany. During the project, the consortium developed and validated technical, governance and organizational building blocks aligned with the European Data Spaces Support Centre (DSSC) Blueprint. The pilot has validated key assumptions regarding interoperability, data sharing, semantic enrichment, discovery services and cross-border collaboration. And has demonstrated that local decisions and legislation can become a shared strategic asset for public administrations, citizens, researchers and businesses through a federated, trustworthy and interoperable data space.

At the conclusion of the project, **the consortium considers it essential to enter a structured evaluation and consolidation phase before establishing any permanent operational model**, before moving beyond a purely experimental pilot.

This next phase will focus on assessing the results achieved during the pilot, the value created for participating organizations, and the level of demand from potential future participants.

The consortium will use this period to:

* evaluate the operational, governance and technical lessons learned from the pilot;
* assess which validated use cases provide sufficient value to justify continued investment;
* identify additional participants, stakeholders and use cases that could contribute to and benefit from the data space;
* finetuning the governance arrangements required for a longer-term operational data space with DSSC recommendations;
* assess options for sustainable financing and resource allocation.

At this stage, the consortium can confirm its commitment to exploring and supporting the future development of the DECIDE ecosystem. The precise form, scale and timing of that future evolution will be determined based on the outcomes of the evaluation phase, the engagement of prospective participants and the identification of sustainable operational resources.

### Potential Use Cases

#### Use cases already explored

* Use case with the Flemish Environmental Agency: map local policy goals (specific kind of local decisions) to the policy goals in the Flemish Climate Adaptation Plan of the Flemish Environment Agency with the use of the codelistmappingtool.
* Use case with the Local Products and Services Catalogue in Flanders: directly fetch the information relevant for the products and services descriptions from the local decision and reuse it using the NER and NEL services.
* Use case within the One-Stop-Shop Strategy of the Flemish government: link local decisions to the relevant target groups also reusing the codelistmappingtool.
* The city of Bamberg is planning to make the tools available to other administrative offices within the city, depending on how they can manage their safety regulations
* The city of Ghent: the local annotations on decisions concerning thematical information (realized in the Probe project), and on locations (realized in the DECIDe project) will be published as linked open data. In this way they will become available in the DECIDE dataspace.

Other use case are currently being explored but no commitments so far.

#### Other potential use cases

Other potential use cases might be found within the following areas:

* Policy intelligence: automatic identification of emerging policy trends; innovative local practices; implementation patterns.
* Cross-border policy observation: support municipalities in learning from comparable cities across Europe.
* Regulatory impact assessment: assess potential effects of proposed local decisions through semantic comparison with historical decisions.
* Public transparency: citizen-oriented interfaces providing understandable policy information, geographical visualizations, legislative journey tracing, …
* Compliance monitoring: f.i. Green Deal initiatives, climate adaptation policies, national policy implementation, …
* Marketplace for enrichment services: a potential future ecosystem where participants offer AI annotations, thematic classifications, analytics, specialized datasets, …
